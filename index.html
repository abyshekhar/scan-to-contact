    How ScanToContact works

    Scan a phone number from a QR code, barcode, or printed text, and add it to your phone's contacts.

    1. Tap Scan, Upload, or Voice Note. Point your camera at the number, choose a photo, or speak the details aloud.
    2. Capture the QR code, barcode, printed digits, or your spoken words.
    3. Check the result. Fix the number if needed, and add a name, email, or company.
    4. Tap "Save to Contacts."
    5. Finish in the Contacts app that opens, and tap Save there.

    Voice notes: say things naturally, like "This is Alex Rivera from Rivera Consulting, my number is 555-222-3333." (Tap "Hear an example" on that screen if you'd like it read aloud first.) Voice notes need a browser with built-in speech recognition (Chrome on Android) — not available on every browser yet.

    Heads up: after tapping "Save to Contacts," your phone's own Contacts app will open automatically. That's expected — this app can't save contacts directly, so you confirm and save the last step yourself.

    Add to your home screen: On iPhone: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen." On Android: tap the menu (⋮) and choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app."

    Privacy: everything happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
